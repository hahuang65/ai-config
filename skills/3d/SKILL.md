# 3D design

Guide one physical part from requirements to adjustable source, printable exports, reviewed renders, and a documented 3D print project.
This is a standalone workflow, not a software implementation sequence.
Use it for brackets, mounts, cases, holders, adapters, and enclosures, not digital-only art or editing an STL without source.

Read and follow the [3D design workflow](references/workflow.md).
Read [FDM design guidance](references/fdm-design.md) before dimension, geometry, material, orientation, or validation decisions.
Read the [model review sheet contract](references/model-review-sheet.md) before presenting evidence for feedback or approval.
Use local [model-domain](../model-domain/SKILL.md) for agreed terms and durable decisions, [review-artifact](../review-artifact/SKILL.md) for HTML review, and [work-log](../work-log/SKILL.md) for meaningful checkpoints without duplicate entries.
Do not claim that computed validation or visual approval proves physical fit, printer accuracy, strength, or safe use.
